The room view: read-only sensors shrink to a compact strip on top, things you control are grouped under small type labels, and search is always visible. Works with A (as what a room pill shows) or with B (as what a room card opens).

They combine

A and B are two answers to “what does Home show first”. C is “what does one room look like” and pairs with either. My pick: A + C. It fixes the flat grid and the sensor clutter while keeping one-tap access to every device, and it builds on the pills you already have.
